# GIS / AI Workflow Brief

Use this template when you want a first proof scoped around a real workflow instead of a vague discovery call.

## 1. Workflow or Decision

- What workflow, handoff, or decision needs to improve?
- Who uses it now?
- What happens when it goes well?
- What breaks, stalls, or gets repeated by hand?

## 2. Current Operating Surface

- Maps, datasets, systems, dashboards, reports, or tools involved
- ArcGIS / AGOL / Portal / database / API context
- Existing screenshots, links, exports, or examples
- Update cadence and who owns the data

## 3. Trust and Constraints

- What would make the output trustworthy enough to use?
- What cannot break?
- Privacy, security, or approval boundaries
- Time, budget, or review constraints that matter now

## 4. First Proof Target

- What should a credible first proof answer in 2-4 weeks?
- What is the smallest useful artifact: map, dashboard, workflow tool, report, benchmark, or review loop?
- What decision should be clearer at the end: build forward, pause, or harden first?

## 5. Send With It

- Links to relevant systems or repos
- Screenshots or sample files
- Any current manual steps worth replacing
- Deadlines or events that change priority
